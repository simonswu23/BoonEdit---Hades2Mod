# Fully removed and fully reworked

Two things worth reading before touching a mod that edits this content: whole boons/hammer
upgrades that were cut outright, and ones that survived under the same internal name but do a
genuinely different thing now -- not a retuned number, a different mechanic.

This file is curated by hand, not regenerated by a script. `reworked.py` narrows the CHANGED list
down to entries whose implementation is mostly different text (`difflib` similarity below 0.55)
and dumps each one's original-vs-current source to `_reworked_candidates.json` for reading; from
the 34 candidates it found, 26 turned out to be an actual mechanic swap and 8 were a false
positive -- same mechanic, but so much new per-weapon-aspect FX or anti-spam plumbing got bolted
on around it that the text similarity looks low anyway (Flutter Flourish (`AphroditeSpecialBoon`),
Ice Flourish (`DemeterSpecialBoon`), Inverted Blaze (`TorchEnhancedAttackTrait`), Volcanic Flourish
(`HephaestusSpecialBoon`), Volcanic Strike (`HephaestusWeaponBoon`), Flame Flourish
(`HestiaSpecialBoon`), Wave Flourish (`PoseidonSpecialBoon`), Heaven Flourish (`ZeusSpecialBoon`)
-- these are all still just numeric-tuned in `CHANGES.md`). The 0.55 cutoff is generous on purpose:
it's tuned to over-catch candidates for a
human to read, not to be a final answer, so a rework that kept more of its surrounding boilerplate
intact than that could still be missing from this list. Rerun `reworked.py` after pulling a new
build and read its output the same way to extend this.

## Hammer upgrades

### Staff
**Removed** (no in-game name survives for either -- cut before they got player-facing text):
- `StaffReserveManaBoostTrait` -- converted unspent (reserved) mana into bonus damage on Staff's
  secondary weapons.
- `StaffSlowExTrait` -- added flat damage to the EX combo finisher at the cost of a slower fire
  rate; required not already having the attack-recovery hammer upgrade.

### Dagger
**Removed** (no in-game name for either):
- `DaggerRepeatStrikeTrait` -- made the multi-stab attack fully automatic on a hold, instead of a
  fixed combo.
- `DaggerSpecialRangeTrait` -- extended the dagger-throw's range to 1400 with bonus damage scaling
  by distance to target; the bonus reverted while charging.

### Torch
**Removed** (no in-game name for any of these):
- `TorchConsecutiveStrikeTrait` -- the EX orbit projectile dealt bonus damage per consecutive hit
  within a 1s window.
- `TorchHomingAttackTrait` -- torch balls turned toward targets much faster and flew faster.
- `TorchOrbitDistanceTrait` -- doubled the EX orbit's range and added flat bonus damage to EX
  attacks.
- `TorchSpinAttackAltTrait` -- an alternate spin version of the basic throw: faster, tighter spin,
  shorter fuse, flat bonus damage, with the fuse extended further if you also held one of the
  Torch aspects.

**Melting Helix (`TorchSpecialLineTrait`)** -- was a % of the target's current health as bonus
damage (an execute). Now a flat bonus damage vs. armored enemies instead.

### Axe
**Removed:** `AxeComboSwingTrait` (no in-game name) -- sped up the axe's third swing into the
special attack if you fired it quickly after the second swing.

### Lob
**Removed:** `LobSpecialAspect` (no in-game name) -- just a `LobExSpecialRecall` flag with no other
data in this build, so its actual behavior (likely pulling the EX Special projectile back to you)
lived in code outside `TraitData_Lob.lua`.

**Fetching Array (`LobAmmoMagnetismTrait`)** -- used to pull nearby ammo but cost you a magazine
slot (`ReduceMaxAmmo`). Now costs nothing; instead the pull *speed* scales with rarity, and it
turns itself off if you're running the Gun Aspect.

**Boosted Ignition (`LobStraightShotTrait`)** -- used to make your charged shot fly straighter and
faster plus add flat bonus damage. Now does neither -- dashing grants a bonus volley of shots
(`CheckDashVolley`) instead.

### Suit
**Removed:** Nirvana Engine (`SuitComboThresholdTrait`) *(last seen: Unseen Update)* -- raised the
execute threshold of Aspect of Nirvana's combo finisher by 5 percentage points, reading the
aspect's own threshold and duration for its tooltip. Only offered while carrying that aspect, so
it went when the aspect's combo mechanic was reworked.

## Boons

### Apollo
**Removed:**
- Critical Miss (`ApolloMissStrikeBoon`) *(last seen: Olympic Update)* -- on a dash, fired a free
  `ApolloPerfectDashStrike` projectile at any foe currently affected by a Blind-genus status. Ran
  once per dash on a 0.2s cooldown, with diminishing returns on repeats against the same target
  (each identical hit at -50%, tapering by 0.8, floored at 10% damage). Apollo's payoff for
  stacking Daze -- the same pairing his Dazzling Display still gestures at.

**Exceptional Talent (`DoubleExManaBoon`)** -- used to just add 2 projectile waves to every EX
attack for +20 mana. Now it's element-swapped (Air -> Fire), costs 30 mana, has per-weapon-aspect
branches (different bonuses if you're running Lob's Gun Aspect, a Suit-specific revert, etc.), and
runs its own setup function -- the flat universal bonus became a web of special cases.

### Ares
**Grisly Gain (`AresManaBoon`)** -- used to refill mana in an emergency near-death moment. Now it's
a 20% per-hit chance to spawn a mana pickup ("blood drop") instead -- no more near-death trigger.

**Sanguinary Savor (`DoubleBloodDropBoon`)** -- used to just double blood-drop pickup value. Now
grants flat bonus damage to a "Blood Moon Beam" projectile and reads a new Ares curse effect's
duration/power -- points to a broader redesign of Ares around a curse-application mechanic rather
than a pickup-value boost.

### Chaos
**Removed** (both last seen at EA launch -- the plain stat blessings, dropped once Chaos' pool
moved to mechanics rather than flat numbers):
- Prowess (`ChaosWeaponBaseBlessing`) -- +10 to +14 base damage on primary weapons.
- Finesse (`ChaosSpecialBaseBlessing`) -- +15 to +20 base damage on secondary weapons. Both rolled
  a value in that range on pickup rather than scaling by rarity.

**Addled (`ChaosCastCurse`)** -- used to add fire-weapon damage to all ranged weapons declaratively.
Now it's an explicit function hook scoped to the Cast weapon only -- same damage numbers, narrower
target and a different implementation shape.

**Fixated (`ChaosManaFocusCurse`)** -- used to do literally nothing beyond the base curse template
(no unique body at all). Now it removes reserved mana on expiry -- functionality added where there
was none.

**Slothful (`ChaosSpeedCurse`)** -- used to apply fixed sprint-speed penalties (multipliers on top
of a separate movement-speed slow). Now it drops the fixed sprint penalty entirely and derives a
sprint slow from the general speed-slow value instead, so the two scale together rather than
stacking as flat multipliers.

### Hephaestus
**Removed:**
- Tough Trade (`ChargeCounterBoon`) *(last seen: EA launch)* -- taking damage empowered your next
  hit with a primary or secondary weapon (`PrepOnHitEmpower`, with an Eris power-up flash). Worth
  +25% on the first stack, then +15% and +10% for the second and third -- a counterattack boon
  paying you for getting hit rather than for avoiding it.
- `HeavyArmorExpired` (no in-game name -- `Hidden = true` and nothing else; an internal marker for
  when a related heavy-armor buff had run out, not a pickable boon)

**Security System (`EncounterStartDefenseBuffBoon`)** -- used to grant temporary invulnerability at
the start of an encounter. Now grants temporary armor (a depletable shield) instead of true
invulnerability.

**Smithy Rush (`HephaestusSprintBoon`)** -- used to replace your dash with a hold-to-charge attack.
Now your dash is untouched, and finishing a dash auto-fires a projectile on a cooldown instead.

### Hera
**Removed:**
- Keen Intuition (`FullManaExBoostBoon`) *(last seen: EA launch)* -- an Omega attack fired at full
  Magick dealt +30% damage. Tracked by flagging the weapon on fire (`CheckWeaponFullManaFire`) and
  clearing it when the projectile died, so the bonus rode the shot rather than the moment.
  Repeats against the same target fell off by half.
- Brave Face (`HeraManaShieldBoon`) *(last seen: EA Patch 4)* -- a Legendary. Redirected 50% of
  incoming damage into Magick, burning 5 Magick per point of damage blocked. Could not be offered
  alongside Hephaestus' own Magick boon. **BoonEdits reimplements this one** -- see
  `mod.tuning.BraveFace`.
- Family Trade (`SwapBonusBoon`) *(last seen: EA Patch 4)* -- one use: forced your next boon offer
  to be a swap (`ForceSwaps`), and the replacement arrived 2 levels higher than the boon it
  displaced. Could not be stacked.
- `ReserveReductionBoon` (no in-game name -- reduced reserved mana cost by 20%, but the source
  comments it as "Unused" even in the build where it's last seen)

**Bridal Glow (`BoonDecayBoon`)** -- total redesign. Used to be an Earth boon that permanently
downgraded two of your other god traits every few rooms (a curse-shaped "boon decay"). Now it's a
Water boon that makes a qualifying Hephaestus ability (Weapon/Special/Sprint, whichever has 2s+
cooldown) "superchargeable," reducing its cooldown further with stacks -- unrelated mechanic, only
the internal name survived.

**Extended Family (`DamageShareRetaliateBoon`)** -- used to reflect a percentage of incoming damage
back at attackers (retaliate-on-hit). Now grants bonus damage per unique Olympian god you have
active in your build -- a build-diversity reward, no retaliation left at all.

### Hermes
**Removed:**
- Midnight Oil (`HexCooldownBuffBoon`) *(last seen: Olympic Update)* -- Hex recharged 15% faster.
  Only offered once you had a Hex slotted, and never alongside the Hex-potion trait.
- Split Second (`PerfectDodgeSlowBoon`) *(last seen: Olympic Update)* -- a perfect dodge slowed
  time to 10% speed for 1 second, on a 4s cooldown. Once per dash.
- `BonusDashBoon` (no in-game name -- gave an extra charge to your dash-type weapons, i.e. one more
  dash before needing to recover)

**Winner's Circle (`HermesCastDiscountBoon`)** -- used to cut Cast's mana cost in half. Now leaves
mana cost alone and instead shortens Cast's charge time and increases cast attack speed.

**Paid Dues (`TimeStopLastStandBoon`)** -- used to be a last-stand: heal + slow time for 8s when
you'd otherwise die. Now converts your gold into a damage shield (`MoneyShieldData`) -- no more
last-stand or time-slow.

### Hestia
**Removed:** Burnt Offering (`SacrificeBoon`) *(last seen: Warsong Update)* -- a meta-currency
boon rather than a combat one. Handed you an Ashes drop on pickup, and thereafter every Ashes you
collected also raised Max Magick by 5. Only offered once you had earned 25 Ashes lifetime, and
could not be stacked. **MoreDuosMod carries a boon under this name** -- see its `Debug.BurntOffering`.

**Fire Away (`BurnSprintBoon`) and Heat Rush (`HestiaSprintBoon`) swapped mechanics with each
other.** Fire Away used to spawn trailing fire puddles while you dashed (with reduced self-damage
from fire); Heat Rush used to build burn stacks off blocking and release them defensively. Now Fire
Away has the block-triggered burn defense (plus a new Cast-weapon variant) and Heat Rush has the
dash fire-puddle trail. Same two effects exist, just relabeled onto the other boon.

**Cardio Gain (`HestiaManaBoon`)** -- used to be passive mana regen paid for with a permanent 20%
max-health cut. Now grants mana on hit instead, with no health tradeoff at all.

### Poseidon
**Removed:**
- Sunken Treasure (`MinorLootBoon`) *(last seen: EA launch)* -- paid out on pickup rather than
  doing anything in combat: 90 gold, two minor healing drops, and a 50% shot at each of a Bones
  and an Ashes drop (both suppressed in Story rooms). Unstackable, could not be rarified or
  sacrificed, and was withheld in rooms flagged against gift boons.
- Crashing Wave (`SlamExplosionBoon`) *(last seen: Olympic Update)* -- knocking a foe into a wall
  or another foe detonated a `PoseidonCollisionBlast` at 3000 force, on a 0.65s cooldown.
  Repeat blasts on the same target fell off by half. Poseidon's knockback finally paying damage.

**Tidal Ring (`PoseidonCastBoon`)** -- used to detonate your Cast for a fuse-timed burst-damage
multiplier. Now fires splash-splinter projectiles when Cast is fired and separately applies a
knockback-amplify effect on hit -- different projectile, different trigger, different secondary
effect.

**Flood Gain (`PoseidonManaBoon`)** -- used to have a chance on hit to drop a mana pickup. Now
grants a periodic (~8s) window where casting is free plus occasional mana refunds -- a timed buff
instead of a drop-chance proc.

### Zeus
**Removed:** `ZeusExCastBoon` (no in-game name -- on Cast detonation, struck nearby enemies
repeatedly with lightning on a short interval, each successive strike weaker; only offered if you
didn't already have Poseidon's or Apollo's EX Cast boon)

**Ionic Gain (`ZeusManaBoon`)** -- used to be passive mana regen paid for with a permanent max-mana
cut, same shape as Cardio Gain's old design. Now periodically spawns a mana pickup near you that
you have to walk over to collect, with no tradeoff.

### Duo
**Removed** (all nine last seen at EA launch -- the duo roster was rebuilt wholesale after it):
- Funeral Pyre (`BurnOmegaBoon`, Hera x Hestia) -- holding an Omega charge burned everything within
  415 units, applying 90 stacks of Burn per second from 0.15s into the charge. Charging itself
  became the attack.
- Torrential Downpour (`CastRampBoon`, Apollo x Demeter) -- each Omega Cast in a row dealt +20%
  more damage than the last, at +5 Magick cost per cast. A ramp you paid for as it climbed.
- Freezer Burn (`DoubleBurnBoon`, Demeter x Hestia) -- applying Burn to a Chilled foe multiplied
  it, and raised Burn's stack ceiling by 500. The two statuses fed each other instead of competing.
- Apocalyptic Storm (`EchoAllBoon`, Demeter x Zeus) -- extended the Echo effect's duration by 8
  seconds, against a base measured in the low single digits.
- Spiteful Strength (`EmptySlotDamageBoon`, Hephaestus x Hera) -- **+200% damage** on any weapon
  whose boon slot you had left empty, checked per slot (Melee for primary, Secondary for special).
  Deliberately going without, paid at triple rate.
- Soft Caress (`FirstHitHealBoon`, Aphrodite x Hephaestus) -- the first hit you landed on a foe
  healed you for 1% of max health.
- Stellar Slam (`MassiveAoEIncrease`, Apollo x Hephaestus) -- Hephaestus' massive-blast finishers
  covered 50% more area.
- Soul Mate (`MaximumShareBoon`, Aphrodite x Hera) -- foes sharing damage took 20% more from you,
  and applying Weak linked up to 2 of them together.
- Burning Desire (`ShadeMercFireballBoon`, Aphrodite x Hestia) -- required the Shade Mercenaries
  world upgrade: +12 to your shade count, and their fireballs hit for 4x. **BoonEdits reimplements
  this one** -- see `mod.tuning.BurningDesire`.

**Natural Selection (`GoodStuffBoon`, Demeter x Poseidon)** -- used to boost your odds of rarer boon
offers. Now grants a flat +8 levels distributed across your Secondary/Rush/Ranged/Melee/Mana
boons instead -- a level-up, not a luck buff.

**Seismic Servo (`MassiveCastBoon`, Hephaestus x Poseidon)** -- used to add a periodic massive-blast
finisher to Cast hits (the same "MassiveAttack" pattern as the Hephaestus weapon boons). Now it's
just a flat Olympian-ability recharge-speed multiplier -- no more Cast finisher.

### Aspect
**Removed** (no in-game name for either):
- `LobSpecialAspect` -- same bare `LobExSpecialRecall` flag as the Lob hammer-upgrade entry above.
- `TorchSingleStrikeAspect` -- reworked Torch's fire-rate/refresh behavior, gave the EX Special a
  charge-stage progression from 2 up to 5 projectiles, and converted torch-ball hits into
  follow-up "repeat strike" hits.

**Aspect of Melinoë (`AxeRecoveryAspect`)** -- used to shorten Axe's attack-recovery/disable
windows (faster follow-up after swings). Now grants flat bonus damage on non-EX attacks plus flat
max health instead -- a stat stick, not a recovery-speed aspect.

**Aspect of Momus (`StaffSelfHitAspect`)** -- used to be a self-damage-punish gimmick: bonus damage
plus a heal if your own charged Staff ball hurt you below 50% health. Now drops stationary "origin
markers" that repeatedly strike an area three times on a timer -- unrelated to self-damage.

**Aspect of Eos (`TorchSprintRecallAspect`)** -- used to make your thrown Torch balls boomerang
back to you (extended fuse, return-to-owner, lifetime-based bonus damage). Now replaces Torch's
whole charge behavior with a different projectile and a reworked EX charge stage (new graphics,
new spread) -- a full weapon-form redesign rather than a projectile-behavior tweak.
